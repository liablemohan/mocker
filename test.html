Navigation & Marking

  • Click an option to select your answer. Click the same option again to deselect.
  • Use Save & Next to confirm your answer and move forward.
  • Use Previous to navigate back — your answer is retained.
  • Click any numbered button in the Question Palette (right side) to jump directly.
  • Use Mark for Review to flag uncertain questions — they appear in purple.
  • Use Clear Response to remove a selected answer anytime.
