General Instructions

Test Details

    Navigation

    • Click an option to select your answer. Click again to deselect.
    • Use Save & Next to confirm and proceed to the next question.
    • Use Previous to go back and change your answer any time.
    • Click any number in the Question Palette to jump directly to that question.
    • Use Mark for Review to flag a question to revisit later.
    • Use Clear Response to remove your selected answer.

    After the Test

    • You can upload a separate answer key file after submission for auto-scoring.
    • You can also download a PDF response sheet with all your answers.

    Question Palette Legend

    1
    Not Visited — you haven't opened this question yet.
    2
    Not Answered — visited but no answer selected.
    3
    Answered — you have selected an answer.
    4
    Marked for Review — flagged, no answer selected.
    5
    Answered & Marked — answered and flagged.

    Important

    • The timer starts the moment you click Start Test.
    • The test enters full-screen mode. Exiting will generate a warning.
    • Switching tabs will be detected and logged.
    • Do not refresh the page — your progress will be lost.
    • The test auto-submits when time runs out.
